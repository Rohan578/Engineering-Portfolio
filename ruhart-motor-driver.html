RU-HART Motor Driver V1

Tank-Drive Motor Controller · RU HART Extension

Proof of Concept

Before designing a board, I built a breadboard proof of concept: a Raspberry Pi Pico driving a discrete MOSFET H-bridge, powered through a boost converter, spinning a small DC motor. This validated the switching circuit and the control logic, and showed the motor could be driven in both directions at variable speed.

Schematic

The V1 schematic turns the proof of concept into a full tank-drive controller. An RP2040 handles control, and a dedicated gate driver switches power MOSFETs for each motor channel. An MCP2515 CAN controller with a CAN transceiver lets the board take commands over the robot's CAN bus. Onboard regulation provides the logic rails, and XT60 connectors handle battery and motor power.
