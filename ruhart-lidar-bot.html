RU-HART Prototype Version 1

LiDAR Mapping Bot (Maxbot)

LiDAR Bot

The first RU-HART prototype is a four-wheel-drive rover with a 360° LiDAR scanner mounted on a raised mast above the chassis. The prototype was built to test the core autonomy stack early: scanning the surroundings, building a picture of nearby obstacles, and driving the motors in response. Lessons from this build fed directly into the custom motor driver and the next version of the robot.
